Welcome to My IMT 561 Portfolio

Samantha Wang

I am a master’s student in Information Management at the University of Washington, focusing on Data Science and Business Intelligence.

This quarter, I focus on designing time visualizations that are intuitive, engaging, and easy to interpret.

I chose working contexts because I often lose track of time during long focus sessions and wanted to design clocks that make time awareness clearer and less stressful.

Documenting iterative process

Context of use: This clock is designed for meeting-heavy workdays. It helps users quickly understand workload without reading individual events. I chose this because I often need a fast way to see when my day becomes busy.

Final Clock 1 — Meeting Wave Clock

Design process: I explored representing meetings as a continuous wave instead of separate blocks. After feedback, I simplified the shape, improved color contrast, and clarified the time axis. I applied readability, hierarchy, and color principles to improve scanning.

Self-reflection / future work: I would connect the clock to real calendar data and add interaction such as hover details to show specific meetings.

Context of use: This clock is designed for focused work sessions. It helps users stay aware of time without stress. I chose this because I often lose track of time while working.

Final Clock 2 — Cat & Fish Clock

Design process: I designed time as disappearing fish with a moving cat. After feedback, I improved layout, enlarged the timer, and simplified visuals. I applied hierarchy, layout, and motion to make time easier to understand.

Self-reflection / future work: I would improve animation smoothness and add labels to make time mapping clearer.
